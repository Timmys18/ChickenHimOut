# Privacy

ChickenHimOut does not require an account and does not collect, sell, or transmit personal data.

Game progress, settings, scores, and unlocked cosmetics are stored locally on the player's device. The share button opens the device share sheet or copies a challenge link only when the player asks it to. The release candidate includes no advertising, analytics, tracking, social-login, or payment SDK.

Uninstalling the app or resetting progress removes locally stored game data, subject to normal device backup behavior.

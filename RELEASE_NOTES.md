# ChickenHimOut 0.9.0-rc.1

## Candidate scope

- Complete 15-mission campaign from **The Greatest Wall** through **The Golden Escalator**.
- Immediate first-run tutorial, guaranteed meaningful first collision, and an attainable first successful escape.
- Physics-based elastic tie, trajectory preview, promise targets, combo scoring, accountability window, bailout cinematic, particles, haptics, and procedural sound.
- Hall of Achievements, campaign map, records, best scores, 45-chicken completion track, six unlockable tie styles, and CLOUT economy.
- Daily Disaster rotation with local streak and rewards.
- Endless Chicken Golf with randomized holes, tie controls, score persistence, and comic cheating assistant.
- Shareable score challenge URLs with direct mission launch.
- English, Russian, Spanish, Portuguese, French, and German core UI.
- Installable offline PWA plus synchronized Android and iOS Capacitor projects.
- Branded icons and native splash screens.

## Deliberately outside the binary

Store signing identities, store listings, product IDs for paid purchases, remote analytics, and multiplayer servers require publisher accounts or external infrastructure. The candidate does not simulate these integrations or collect user data.

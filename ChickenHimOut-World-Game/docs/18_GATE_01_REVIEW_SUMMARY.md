## Core Wall Scenario

The player lifts the final wall panel. The crane's auto-level system rotates it away from the gap. The player must use either the moving money truck as a counterweight or an improvised billboard brace. Once the panel is stable for the official photo, the rejected giant bill snaps back and destabilizes the solution. The player chooses between preserving the real structure and forcing the payment truck into the television frame, then actively escapes through the collapse.

## Why This Avoids the First Iteration

The mission cannot end after the first pull. The first Commit creates a new problem. The world responds through mechanics, NPCs, sound and camera. The player makes multiple subsequent decisions, and the escape uses the exact consequences created during play.

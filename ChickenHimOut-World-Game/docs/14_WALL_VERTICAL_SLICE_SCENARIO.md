# The Wall — Vertical Slice Scenario v1

**Status:** DRAFT — Gate 01  
**Role:** first mechanical prototype and first production vertical slice

## 1. Premise

На международно узнаваемой строительной площадке герой должен установить «последнюю, идеальную секцию» огромной стены к началу телевизионной церемонии и добиться кадра, в котором соседняя сторона якобы оплачивает проект.

Тема считывается без имён и длинных объяснений:

- гигантская недостроенная стена;
- разрыв между секциями;
- золотой счёт со стрелкой `PAY HERE`;
- грузовик с деньгами на другой стороне;
- пресс-сцена и преждевременная ленточка;
- герой с жёлтым силуэтом волос и длинным красным галстуком.

## 2. Unique Mechanical Thesis

> Балансировать массивную конструкцию, пока несколько систем пытаются компенсировать нагрузку каждая по-своему.

Это миссия про противовесы, угол тяги, временные опоры и момент отпускания — не про попадание снарядом в стену.

## 3. Objective

Primary:

- установить final panel в проём;
- удержать её в «достаточно установленном» состоянии до официального фото;
- покинуть сцену после начала системного обрушения.

Optional:

- получить payment photo;
- сохранить пресс-сцену;
- не разрушить соседние пролёты;
- забрать золотой счёт;
- выполнить длинную Chaos Chain.

## 5. Tactical Positions

### P0 — Press Stage

Safe start. Good view, weak pull angle. Access:

- bill mechanism;
- ribbon rig;
- temporary brace release.

### P1 — Crane Platform

Strong control over crane and panel. Access:

- crane hook;
- boom routing point;
- counterweight latch;
- emergency brake.

### P2 — Wall Base

High risk, best precision. Access:

- panel lower anchor;
- brace socket;
- gate cable;
- hidden absurd route.

### P3 — Billboard Scaffold

Risky elevated point. Access:

- billboard support;
- balloon/escape anchor;
- long spectacular linkage.

## 6. World Processes Before Player Action

- crane slowly cycles through calibration motion;
- money truck approaches payment gate, stops when bill scanner rejects it, reverses and repeats;
- workers align small parts and move away;
- press cameras track the hero;
- wind periodically pushes the suspended ribbon and flags;
- final panel remains on cradle;
- ceremony countdown advances;
- auto-level system monitors crane balance.

At least one useful timing window exists: the truck briefly aligns with counterweight cable every cycle.

## 7. Main Interactive Entities

### Final Panel

- gameplay mass class: Massive;
- states: Cradled → Suspended → Aligned → Braced → Photo Stable → Failing → Fallen;
- upper and lower tie anchors;
- controlled modular break points.

### Primary Crane

- boom angle;
- hook position;
- counterweight state;
- auto-level response;
- emergency brake;
- overload state.

### Counterweight Block

- Heavy/Massive depending on connection;
- can be linked to crane, truck or brace;
- moves on short rail.

### Money Truck

- autonomous route;
- rejected by payment gate;
- can become moving counterweight;
- can carry hero during escape;
- payment cargo is stylized, not real currency branding.

### Bill Mechanism

- prints/extends giant bill;
- bill returns like a physical accordion after rejection;
- can act as sail, brace or escape ribbon;
- creates readable satire through physical function.

### Temporary Brace

- supports panel for a limited load;
- visible stress states;
- can be installed cleanly or improvised with billboard.

### Billboard Scaffold

- reactive structure;
- optional support/ramp;
- spectacular route component;
- final escape anchor.

## 8. Mission Phases

### Phase 0 — Establishing

Duration: 2–3 seconds.

Camera shows:

1. incomplete wall gap;
2. final panel;
3. money truck rejected by bill gate;
4. hero arriving at press stage.

On-screen objective uses minimal text/iconography:

`INSTALL FINAL PANEL`

Secondary icon:

`GET THEM TO PAY`

### Phase 1 — First Intervention

Player chooses P1 or remains at P0.

Recommended onboarding route:

- move to P1;
- Hook final panel upper anchor;
- Link panel to crane hook;
- Pull counterweight latch to begin lift.

The panel rises. This is Commit. Mission cannot complete here.

### Phase 2 — System Response

As panel lifts:

- crane auto-level detects imbalance;
- boom rotates away from the gap;
- counterweight rail shifts;
- panel begins to yaw;
- workers retreat;
- press incorrectly starts applause;
- countdown accelerates.

New decision windows appear:

- link truck to counterweight when it enters alignment window;
- move to P2 and stabilize lower panel anchor;
- use billboard as improvised brace.

### Phase 3 — Escalation A: Alignment

Player must choose one of two primary routes.

#### Safe Route

1. Link money truck to counterweight rail.
2. Wait until truck moves forward.
3. Release emergency brake at correct moment.
4. Move to P2.
5. Pull lower panel anchor into brace socket.
6. Activate temporary brace.

Result: panel enters `Photo Stable` with limited collateral.

#### Spectacular Route

1. Move to P3.
2. Link billboard scaffold to panel lower anchor.
3. Use crane rotation to pull billboard upright as improvised brace.
4. Release panel at peak swing.
5. Billboard collapses into place and wedges panel.

Result: longer chain, higher Chaos/Style, unstable press area.

### Phase 4 — Payment Gag / Pressure Choice

Once panel is photo-stable, ceremony starts automatically.

The giant bill extends toward the money truck. The payment gate rejects it and the bill snaps back, creating a new force that destabilizes either:

- the temporary brace on Safe Route;
- the billboard support on Spectacular Route.

Player chooses:

- secure the panel and abandon payment photo;
- redirect bill around routing point to pull truck into frame;
- use returning bill as escape line.

This is the explicit pressure choice: preserve real stability or improve the television image.

### Phase 5 — False Success Beat

If the player gets the truck into the marked photo zone:

- flashbulbs fire;
- hero poses;
- `MISSION ACCOMPLISHED?` beat appears briefly;
- panel stress rises behind him;
- NPCs applaud too early.

The photo is a gameplay checkpoint, not final completion.

### Phase 6 — Collapse and Active Escape

The original press-stage exit is blocked by:

- rolling counterweight;
- collapsed ribbon rig;
- returning bill;
- moving truck or falling billboard.

Two escape options:

#### Primary Escape — Golf Cart Tow

- Hook passing golf cart or service cart;
- use tie as tow line;
- release after clearing falling panel shadow.

#### Spectacular Escape — Balloon/Bill Swing

- move to P3 or catch billboard anchor;
- route tie through balloon mast;
- use returning bill force to swing over wall;
- land on money truck or safe platform.

Escape must be actively timed. A simple tap on `Exit` is forbidden.

## 9. Hidden Absurd Route

At P2, player can pull the lower panel past its intended socket so it rotates horizontally and bridges the gap like a giant gate.

The objective detector accepts the gap as `closed`, the ceremony starts, and vehicles immediately use the panel as a ramp. This produces:

- formal Objective success;
- maximum Absurd Style;
- heavy Collateral;
- severe Prize Probability penalty.

## 10. Chaos Graph — Critical Chain

Core nodes:

1. `PanelHooked`
2. `CounterweightReleased`
3. `PanelLifted`
4. `AutoLevelTriggered`
5. `PanelYawUnstable`
6. `TruckAlignmentWindow`
7. `TruckLinked` OR `BillboardLinked`
8. `PanelAligned`
9. `BraceActivated`
10. `PhotoStable`
11. `CeremonyStarted`
12. `BillRejected`
13. `ReturnForceTriggered`
14. `SupportFailing`
15. `EscapeAnchorsEnabled`
16. `HeroEscaped`
17. `FinalCollapse`
18. `Result`

At least two player-created branches occur after `PanelLifted`.

## 11. Fail States

### Panel Drop Before Alignment

Panel falls away from gap. Short hero reaction, quick retry.

### Crane Overload

Incorrect high tension breaks hook/boom safety and drops panel into cradle area.

### Hero Trapped

Player remains at P2 through collapse without attaching escape line.

### Objective Destroyed

Final panel breaks into non-recoverable state before photo checkpoint.

### Timeout

Ceremony begins with empty gap. Press cameras turn toward hero as backdrop collapses. Retry is immediate.

## 12. Scoring

### Objective

- panel in gap;
- photo checkpoint;
- payment truck in frame;
- hero escaped.

### Chaos

Unique system chain:

- crane;
- counterweight;
- truck;
- bill mechanism;
- brace/billboard;
- ceremony rig;
- escape vehicle.

### Style

- precise truck timing;
- minimal reattach count;
- high-tension release window;
- billboard route;
- bill swing escape;
- hidden horizontal-panel route.

### Collateral

- adjacent wall damage;
- press stage damage;
- crane damage;
- worker equipment loss;
- truck cargo loss.

### Prize Probability

Examples:

- clean installation: small increase;
- payment photo achieved through obvious trick: visibility increase, credibility decrease;
- massive collapse after successful photo: high gameplay score, net prize decrease;
- absurd bridge route: legendary style, major probability decrease.

## 13. Visual Direction

- premium stylized 3D, slightly toy-like materials but believable weight;
- broad cinematic desert light;
- red tie is the strongest saturated accent;
- wall geometry has scale, seams, dust and stress response;
- hero and environment share light probes/shadows;
- no painted interactive details in background;
- camera preserves readable silhouettes at peak chaos.

## 14. Audio Beats

- distant construction ambience;
- crane motor and cable tension;
- payment scanner rejection motif;
- accordion-like bill return sound;
- premature applause sting;
- structural stress escalation;
- tie whip and tension layers;
- golf-cart/balloon escape accent;
- final result music adapts to clean/spectacular/absurd outcome.

## 15. Prototype Reduction

Gate 02 greybox does not need full art or all branches.

Required prototype subset:

- one panel;
- one crane arm;
- one counterweight rail;
- looping truck;
- two tactical positions;
- Safe Route;
- one system response;
- one payment-bill destabilization event;
- one active escape;
- Chaos Graph debug overlay.

Spectacular and Hidden Absurd routes are added after basic feel passes.

## 16. Vertical Slice Acceptance

- premise readable internationally without names;
- first interaction understood in under 10 seconds;
- panel lift does not complete mission;
- at least two meaningful decisions after Commit;
- Safe and Spectacular routes feel mechanically different;
- world reacts physically, systemically, socially and audiovisually;
- escape uses mission consequences;
- final scene passes Wallpaper Test;
- stable performance on target mobile tier;
- player voluntarily retries to attempt another route.
